PicklesToys is a one-bench toy workshop in the making. The plan is simple: build sturdy, funny, hand-finished toys a few at a time — the kind that look like they wandered out of a Saturday-morning cartoon and got played with until the paint wore soft.

picklestoys.com is claimed. The toys come next.

The idea

Three things worth committing to

  • 01

    Made by hand

    Cut, sanded, sewn, and painted one at a time. Marks of the hand that made it are the point, not a defect to apologize for.

  • 02

    Small batches

    A handful of pieces per run, then on to the next idea. No warehouse, no endless restocks — when a batch is gone, it is gone.

  • 03

    Built to be wrecked

    These are toys, not shelf pieces. Chunky, drop-tolerant, and finished so a scuff reads as history instead of damage.

The look

Scrappy, wobbly, loud

The design language borrows from early-90s cartoon art direction — the era when kids' TV stopped looking tidy. Lumpy silhouettes. Lines that never quite close. Colors that clash on purpose. Halftone dots and paper grain instead of gradients.

Everything here is drawn from scratch in that spirit. The toys will be original designs and original characters — the influence is the era's attitude, not anybody else's cast.
